##  Real-World AI Applications

### A. ChatGPT / Generative AI

**What it is:** A Large Language Model (LLM) that generates human-like text.

**How it works:**

1. Trained on billions of text pages from the internet
2. Uses **Transformers** — a type of neural network architecture
3. Uses **RLHF** (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) to improve answers
4. Predicts the next word in a sentence, one word at a time

**Real-world uses:**

- Writing emails, essays, code
- Customer support chatbots
- Language translation
- Image generation (DALL-E, Midjourney)

**Key Fact:** ChatGPT has ~800 million weekly active users as of 2026.

### **B. Self-Driving Cars**

**What it is:** Cars that can navigate without human drivers using AI.

**How it works (4 steps):**

1. **Perception** — Cameras, lidar, radar "see" the environment
2. **Localization** — GPS + maps know exactly where the car is
3. **Prediction** — AI predicts what pedestrians/cars will do next
4. **Planning** — AI decides the safest path and controls steering/braking

**Two Major Approaches:**

| Company | Approach | Sensors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waymo** (Google) | Heavy mapping + many sensors | 29 cameras, 6 radars, 5 lidars |
| **Tesla** | Vision-only (cameras only) | 8 cameras, no lidar |

**Autonomy Levels:**

- **L0-L2:** Human drives, AI assists (most cars today)
- **L3:** AI drives, human must be ready to take over
- **L4:** AI drives in specific areas (Waymo robotaxis)
- **L5:** Fully autonomous everywhere (not achieved yet)

### **C. Recommendation Systems**

**What it is:** AI that suggests content/products you might like.

**How it works:**
Two main techniques:

| Technique | How It Works | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Content-Based** | Recommends items similar to what you liked before | "You watched Action Movie A → here's Action Movie B" |
| **Collaborative Filtering** | Recommends what similar users liked | "Users like you also bought..." |

**Real-world examples:**

- **Netflix** — suggests movies based on your watch history
- **Spotify** — creates "Discover Weekly" playlists
- **Amazon** — "Customers who bought this item also bought..."
- **YouTube** — recommends videos to keep you watching

**Why it matters:** Netflix estimates its recommendation system saves **$1 billion per year** by reducing churn.

### **D. AI in Healthcare**

**What it is:** Using AI to improve diagnosis, treatment, and patient care.

**Key Applications:**

| Application | How AI Helps | Real Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Medical Imaging** | Detects diseases in X-rays, MRIs, CT scans faster & more accurately | Google Health AI detects breast cancer in mammograms with 94.5% accuracy |
| **Drug Discovery** | Speeds up finding new medicines by predicting molecular behavior | Insilico Medicine's AI discovered a drug for lung fibrosis in 18 months (vs 4-5 years normally) |
| **Virtual Health Assistants** | Chatbots answer patient questions 24/7 | Babylon Health, Ada Health |
| **Remote Patient Monitoring** | Wearables + AI track vitals and alert doctors | NHS Virtual Wards in the UK |
| **Surgical Robots** | AI-assisted precision surgery | Da Vinci Surgical System |

**Important:** AI **supports** doctors — it does not replace them. Doctors make final decisions.
